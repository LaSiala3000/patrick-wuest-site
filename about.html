About Me

Based in New York

I lead product teams that turn complex models and infrastructure into tools people can rely on.

At Venice AI, I lead product strategy for chat and agents, including how people work with different models, use connectors, and pick up conversations across devices.

Before Venice, I led product teams at Spotify building ML-powered measurement and attribution for advertisers. At Google, I worked on Analytics and conversion measurement, connecting what advertisers could learn about performance with how they optimized campaigns.

I studied economics at the London School of Economics and economics and political science at Sorbonne University. I speak English, French, and German.
